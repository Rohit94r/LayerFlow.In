## Good conversation openers (from research.md Appendix A)

- "Have you ever been surprised by an AI bill?"
- "How do you currently keep track of what AI costs your clients?"
- "What happens today when your spend on OpenAI/Anthropic goes 2× over budget?"
- "Would you hand a per-project cap to a tool if it took 2 minutes to set up?"

## Rules

1. Bank **verbatim quotes** within 30 minutes of the call. Never paraphrase
   later "from memory" and call it a quote.
2. Ask for the **story behind the pain**, not a feature wish list.
3. Every conversation ends with one of: agreement to try the product, an intro,
   or a clear no. "Interested" alone doesn't count.
4. Update the decision dashboard the same week.
