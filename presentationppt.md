# 🎙 SPEECH SCRIPT (Shark Tank Style)

## SLIDE 1 — TITLE (10 sec)
"Good morning. I'm Rohit Jadhav, Founder and CEO of LayerFlow. Our product is live at **layerflow.dev** right now. Today I'll show you the problem, the product, and the business in 12 minutes."

## SLIDE 2 — PROBLEM (1 min)
"Quick question — how many of you use more than one AI tool? ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — raise your hand."

*(Pause 2 seconds)*

"Now here's the real problem.

You save prompts in Notion. Your friend saves them in Google Docs. Someone else has them buried in ChatGPT history. There's no folder structure. No version control. No way to compare what you wrote last week with what you wrote today.

Second — you're paying for multiple AI tools, but nobody tells you which model gives the best output for the lowest cost. The bill just shows up.

Third — you're manually copy-pasting the same prompt into five different tabs to compare outputs. That's not a workflow. That's a headache.

**5.2 million developers in India use multiple AI tools daily. Not one of them has a unified workspace.**"

## SLIDE 3 — SOLUTION (1 min)
"LayerFlow fixes all three problems in one product.

**Rescue and Chat** — you chat across any model. Hit a limit? We extract the context, compress it, and let you continue in another model. Zero re-explaining.

**Prompt Library** — organize prompts into domains, projects, and folders. Every save creates a version. You can roll back to any point instantly.

**Cost Control** — hard budget caps that block requests when you hit them. Not a warning email after the damage. A block before it happens. Plus per-model cost analytics so you know exactly where your money goes.

**BYOK Gateway** — bring your own provider keys. One OpenAI-compatible API for every model. Any developer can point their code at our URL and get budgets, routing, and comparison for free."

## SLIDE 4 — HOW IT WORKS (45 sec)
"The workflow is four steps.

You paste your dead chat — from ChatGPT, Claude, wherever. LayerFlow extracts the full context. We compress it, score it, and recommend the best model for the next run. You continue with complete context in any model.

Three things that matter: rescue a dead conversation in 30 seconds. See exact cost per model before you spend a single rupee. And every prompt you save has full version history — like Git, but for prompts.

It works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, Groq, Grok, and Perplexity. No new tools to learn."

## SLIDE 5 — DEMO (3–4 min)
"Now let me show you the actual product. I'm opening **layerflow.dev** — this is the real, live production site."

*(Walk through: sign in → workspace → create project → save prompt → version timeline → set budget → run compare → cost optimizer → gateway. Narrate every click.)*

"Everything I just showed you is live on production right now. Not a mockup. Not a recording."

## SLIDE 6 — WHAT'S LIVE (45 sec)
"Here's what's live today. Twelve modules, all running in production.

Multi-model chat with auto-switching. Limit rescue — continue after any AI hits its cap. Prompt library with full versioning. Cost analytics with per-model breakdowns. Hard budget caps that actually block. Encrypted BYOK vault. AI memory that searches across sessions. Autonomous agents with approvals. Team workspaces with roles. Terminal sync via CLI. Model comparison side by side. And Improve Prompt — AI scores and rewrites your prompts.

Three of four roadmap phases are complete. Phase four — the SDK and CLI — is in progress."

## SLIDE 7 — TECH (30 sec)
"Behind the product: Next.js frontend, TypeScript end to end, Hono for backend APIs, PostgreSQL on Neon, Redis and BullMQ for background jobs, Better Auth for authentication, and AES-256-GCM encryption for provider keys.

We integrate with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek, Kimi, and xAI. Your keys are encrypted at rest — we never see them in plaintext."

## SLIDE 8 — COMPETITION (45 sec)
"There are existing players — OpenRouter, PromptLayer, browser extensions. But none of them do everything we do.

OpenRouter routes models but has no prompt library. Browser extensions compare outputs but have no cost control. PromptLayer organizes prompts but doesn't rescue dead conversations.

LayerFlow is the only product that combines rescue, prompt versioning, cost control, multi-model comparison, and a BYOK gateway in one workspace. We don't compete with ChatGPT. We sit on top of every model and add what they can't."

Starter at five dollars a month — unlimited chat, AI memory, BYOK, cost analytics, budget caps, prompt library, terminal sync. Right now we're bootstrapped. Zero MRR. Zero paying customers. That's honest.
